Product Classification

Is Your Product Regulated in Saudi Arabia?

Knowing your product's classification under SASO is the first step — it determines which certificate you need and whether testing is required.

Higher Risk

Regulated Products

Products covered under specific Saudi technical regulations — typically electronics, automotive parts, toys, building materials, and personal protective equipment.

  • Required: Product Certificate of Conformity (PCoC), valid for 1 year
  • Plus: Shipment Certificate of Conformity (SCoC) for every consignment
  • Testing: Mandatory third-party test report from an accredited lab
  • Examples: LED lighting, electrical appliances, toys, ACs, cables
Lower Risk

Non-Regulated Products

Products not covered under any specific technical regulation — entry is permitted with simplified self-declaration documentation.

  • Required: Shipment Certificate of Conformity (SCoC) only
  • Basis: Self-declaration by the importer or exporter
  • Testing: No mandatory third-party testing required
  • Validity: Per shipment basis, one SCoC per consignment
Pro Tip: Higher-risk categories like electronics, automotive parts, and children's products almost always require both PCoC and SCoC. If you're unsure where your product falls, our compliance experts can classify it within 30 minutes.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SASO and SABER?+

SASO is the Saudi regulatory authority that sets product standards. SABER is the online platform where exporters and importers register products and obtain the Product CoC (PCoC) and Shipment CoC (SCoC). In short: SASO regulates, SABER is the platform you use to comply.

What is the difference between PCoC and SCoC?+

PCoC is issued for a regulated product after testing and review, valid for one year. SCoC is required for every individual shipment, regardless of whether the product is regulated. Regulated products need both; non-regulated products need only an SCoC.

How long does SASO/SABER certification take?+

With existing accredited test reports, PCoC issuance can be completed in 5–10 working days. Products needing fresh testing may take 3–4 weeks. SCoC for individual shipments is typically issued within 1–2 working days.

Do I need a Saudi importer to apply for SABER?+

Yes. The SABER platform is operated by the Saudi importer or consignee. The exporter typically initiates the PCoC process with a conformity assessment body, while the Saudi importer registers the SCoC per shipment. We coordinate with both parties.

Is third-party testing always required?+

Third-party testing from an accredited lab is mandatory for all regulated products. Non-regulated products generally move forward on a self-declaration basis via SCoC. Our team confirms exactly what your product needs during the initial assessment call.

What if my shipment is urgent?+

For urgent shipments, we expedite testing coordination, document preparation, and SABER submissions to deliver the fastest possible turnaround. Most urgent SCoC requests are turned around within 24–48 hours.
